Last updated: November 10, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Fruits Puzzle Mania ("the App", "we", "our"), an Android game developed by Sujit, handles information when you use the App. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not directly collect your name, email address, phone number or other personal details.

However, the App uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information from your device, such as:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location (country or region)
  • Device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • App usage and crash or diagnostic data

Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to show advertisements. AdMob and other Google services may use the information above to serve and measure ads, which may be personalized based on your interests. These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob and Google Play Services data usage

Ad Personalization and Your Choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization at any time from your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Opting out does not remove ads, but they may be less relevant to you.

How Information Is Used

Information collected by third-party services is used to display ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and keep the App running reliably. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Storage

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App removes this local data.

Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience game and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will work to address it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
